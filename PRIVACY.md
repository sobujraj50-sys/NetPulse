# NetPulse Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 21 November 2026_

What the app reads: cell tower information (operator, cell ID, signal), SIM and network type, GPS location, and data usage counters. This is needed to show network measurements.

Where it is stored: only on your phone — in the app's own storage. Drive-test logs (CSV) and cell names never leave your phone unless you share them yourself.

What is sent over the internet:
• Map tiles are downloaded from OpenStreetMap / OpenTopoMap servers (they see your IP address and the map area you view).
• Only if you add an OpenCelliD API key: the serving cell's MCC, MNC, LAC/TAC and cell ID are sent to opencellid.org to find the tower location.
• Addresses are looked up with Android's built-in geocoder (provided by Google on most phones).

No ads, no analytics, no accounts. No personal data is sold or shared.

Deleting data: clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

Contact: open an issue on the app's GitHub page.
